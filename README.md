# DISC Dubai – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Diversified Integrated Sports Clinic (DISC Dubai) is a multidisciplinary healthcare provider established in 2014 at Dubai Healthcare City. The clinic combines sports medicine, physiotherapy, chiropractic, rehabilitation, diagnostics, preventive healthcare and longevity services.

The redesigned homepage targets athletes, active individuals, patients recovering from injuries, and people seeking long-term health and performance support. The design direction builds on DISC's established integrated healthcare positioning while creating a more distinctive medical editorial experience focused on clinical expertise, patient needs and accessible appointment pathways.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three strategic improvement opportunities identified on the original website:

### 1. Clearer Healthcare Positioning
- Retained the existing headline, "Your Life. In Full Motion."
- Added supporting messaging to explain DISC's integrated healthcare model and its range of medical specialties.
- Made the connection between sports medicine, rehabilitation, physiotherapy and preventive healthcare clearer.

### 2. More Connected Patient Service Discovery
- Improved the connection between body-area concerns, relevant treatments and clinical specialties.
- Created clearer pathways towards relevant practitioner information.
- Made it easier for visitors to move from identifying a health concern to exploring appropriate services and appointment options.

### 3. Better Integrated Booking Information
- Improved the relationship between appointment actions, clinic locations and insurance information.
- Strengthened the visibility and consistency of booking and WhatsApp contact pathways.
- Connected important patient decision-making information more closely with conversion opportunities throughout the homepage.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Introduced a contemporary medical editorial direction combining clinical precision, human-centred photography and movement-inspired visual elements.
- **Layout and Structure:** Improved the organisation of healthcare services, patient pathways and supporting clinic information.
- **User Experience:** Created a more connected journey from healthcare concerns to relevant specialties, practitioners and appointment options.
- **Content Consistency:** Strengthened the relationship between DISC's integrated healthcare positioning and its individual clinical services.
- **Overall Appearance:** Developed a more distinctive and cohesive homepage while preserving DISC's established brand identity and core messaging.

## Project Summary

The DISC Dubai homepage redesign focuses on strengthening healthcare positioning, connecting patient concerns with relevant clinical services and integrating booking information more effectively. Rather than replacing the existing identity, the redesign builds upon DISC's established strengths to create a clearer, more cohesive and patient-focused digital experience.
